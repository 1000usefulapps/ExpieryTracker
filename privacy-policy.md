# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-19

This Privacy Policy describes how **ExpiryTracker** (`com.myhome.expirytracker`) handles information.

## 1) Data you create in the app

ExpiryTracker allows you to create and manage product reminders. Depending on how you use the app, you may store:

- item names and dates
- categories and reminder settings
- optional photos attached to items

This content is primarily stored on your device.

## 2) Notifications and alarms

The app uses Android notification and scheduling features to remind you about expiry dates.

## 3) Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads for free users.
AdMob may collect device and usage data according to Google's policies.

- AdMob / Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4) Purchases

The app offers in-app purchases/subscriptions via Google Play Billing.
Purchase status is validated against Google Play when needed.

## 5) Permissions

The app may request permissions such as:

- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (to show reminders)
- ad-related identifier access via Google Play services (where applicable)

The app does not request unnecessary personal permissions such as contacts or location.

## 6) Backup and restore

Some app data can be included in Android backup/restore according to system settings.
Billing entitlement cache is not used as a backup source of truth.

## 7) Data retention and deletion

You can delete items/photos from within the app.
You can also clear app data or uninstall the app at any time.

## 8) Children

The app is not specifically directed to children under the age required by local law.

## 9) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted in this document with a new "Last updated" date.
