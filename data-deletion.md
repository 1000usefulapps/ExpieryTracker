# Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-04-19

You can delete your data in one of the following ways:

## 1) In-app deletion

- Remove individual items/photos from inside the app.

## 2) Device-level deletion

- Android Settings -> Apps -> ExpiryTracker -> Storage -> Clear data
- or uninstall the app

This removes local app data from your device.

## 3) Server-side deletion requests

ExpiryTracker does not maintain a dedicated user account backend for core item data.
If you have a privacy request, contact:

- 1000usefulapps@gmail.com
